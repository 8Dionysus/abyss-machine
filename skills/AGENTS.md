## Applies to

This card applies to the canonical `abyss-machine/skills/` owner home.

## Role

This home owns admitted callable procedures whose meaning belongs specifically
to `abyss-machine`. The current bundle is
`skills/os-abyss-artifact-trust-loop/`.

to the capability, but it never becomes source truth.

## Read before editing

Read root `AGENTS.md`, `skills/README.md`, `skills/port.manifest.json`, the
target package, and the artifact policy or owner surface that gives the
procedure meaning.

## Boundaries

- Keep owner procedure here and artifact policy, registry logic, CLI behavior,
  manifests, schemas, and generated evidence with their existing owners.
- Do not copy shared `aoa-skills` bundles or create a second `.agents/skills`
  catalog.
- Do not store manual trial prompts, task-local DAGs, temporary registries,
  bundle fixtures, rubrics, live host state, or generated KAG projections here.
- Do not add a callable bundle merely because a command exists. Require an
  independent trigger, contract, composition value, failure modes, and manual
  evidence of benefit.
- Change canonical source first, then reinstall the single OS-user projection
  through the owning `aoa-skills` route.

## Validation

Manual positive, negative, identity-mismatch, access-plane-fallback, and
coexistence tasks decide usefulness. The standard skill shape check and the
repository's existing public/release lanes check structure and owner
integration; they do not prove outcome benefit.

Do not add a validator or test solely to make this package green. Add durable
automation only after repeated manual work reveals a stable invariant whose
false-positive and false-negative boundaries are understood.

## Closeout

Report manual cases, owner surfaces, actual effects, global profile status,
checks skipped, and removal of session-only trial material.
